The Secondary Examination Pathway (Grades 8 to 10)

Grade 8: Basic Level Exam (BLE)

Municipal board examination readiness with full syllabus revision, conceptual tests, and practical science evaluations.

Grade 9: Conceptual Consolidation

Advanced elective choices (Optional Mathematics / Computer), in-depth science laboratory projects, and weekly chapter tests.

Grade 10: SEE Board Mastery

Intensive pre-board examination series, terminal paper analysis, individual doubt clearing, and career guidance for college admissions.
