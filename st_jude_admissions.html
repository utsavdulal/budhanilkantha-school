Admissions Inquiries

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Clarifying standardized testing policies, residential life, waitlist movements, and international visa sponsorship.

school

Which grades are currently open for admission?

Admissions are open from Playgroup (PG) through Grade 10. For PG, Nursery, and Kindergarten, rolling admission is accepted based on age readiness. Grades 1 through 9 are subject to entrance assessment.

hotel

Does the school provide hostel facilities?

Yes. We offer well-maintained, secure hostel accommodation for students with structured study schedules, dedicated teachers for evening tuition, and healthy, hygienic meals prepared in-house.

sports_cricket

What sports and extracurricular activities are offered?

Our students benefit from professional cricket training guided by ICC Panel Umpire Mr. Buddhi Bahadur Pradhan, along with football, taekwondo, robotics, speech, art, music, and international study exposure tours like NASA and Japan programs.

location_on

How can parents visit the school?

Parents can visit our campus located at Shankarpur, Biratnagar-2 during working hours (Sunday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM) or dial 021-514168 to arrange an in-person interaction with our administration.
