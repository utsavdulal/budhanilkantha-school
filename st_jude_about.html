Principal's Message

Cultivating Curiosity, Character, and Global Vision.

“Education is not the mere memorization of syllabus; it is the ignition of innate curiosity, moral integrity, and the confidence to compete globally.”

Welcome to Budhanilkantha Secondary English School (बुढानिलकण्ठ विद्यालय), Shankarpur, Biratnagar-2. Since our humble inception in 2040 B.S. (1983 A.D.), we have embraced one steadfast mission: to create an empowering learning atmosphere where children thrive through personal care, academic discipline, and creative freedom.

Over the past four decades, our students have achieved stellar results in SLC and SEE examinations, while our pioneering co-curricular philosophy has sent student delegations to international stages—including NASA in the United States and Asian Camp in Japan.

We believe that school life must prepare students for real-world leadership. Through child-centered classrooms, robotics instruction, comprehensive sports training under eminent mentors like ICC Panel Umpire Mr. Buddhi Bahadur Pradhan, and rich cultural arts, we guide each young person toward becoming a compassionate, knowledgeable, and capable citizen.

We warmly invite parents and prospective scholars to visit our Shankarpur campus, meet our caring faculty, and become part of our ever-growing family.
