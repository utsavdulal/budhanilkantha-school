WELCOME TO BUDHANILKANTHA SECONDARY ENGLISH SCHOOL

More Than Education. A Foundation for Life & Global Achievement.

Established in 2040 B.S. in Shankarpur, Biratnagar-2, Budhanilkantha Secondary English School delivers benchmark academic rigor paired with co-curricular distinction.
